Clear before commitment

The terms, up front.

Commercial details depend on the product brief. We confirm the specifics in writing before an order is agreed.

01

MOQ

Minimum order quantity is set per SKU and specification, including materials, finish and branding. It must be confirmed in a written quote. Trial or sample quantities may be discussed case by case; they are not guaranteed.

02

OEM / ODM

OEM: production to buyer-approved specifications and branding, after feasibility review and sample/specification approval. ODM: adapting or developing products with Kavora, subject to feasibility and signed-off design and sample.

03

Incoterms & quotation

EXW is the starting quotation basis. FOB and CIF can be requested and confirmed per product and destination. Every quote must state the named place or port and the applicable Incoterms rule and edition.

04

Natural variation

Leather is a natural material, so grain, tone and character can vary. Share the finish and appearance your project needs; the agreed specification and approved sample guide the discussion.

To receive a useful quote: include your company, destination, product and specification, estimated quantity, OEM/ODM requirements and preferred EXW, FOB or CIF basis. Pricing and order details are confirmed directly, in writing.
